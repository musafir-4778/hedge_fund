│     * **The Denominator Trap:** RIL is aggressively expanding its asset     │
│ base via relentless, multi-billion-dollar outlays into New Energy (solar,   │
│ hydrogen, gigafactories) and telecom/retail infrastructure. While top-line  │
│ and net income growth look visually impressive on a percentage basis off    │
│ lower historical bases, the *return generated on every incremental rupee of │
│ equity* is collapsing.                                                      │
│     * **Cost of Capital Reality Check:** If RIL's ROE is stuck at 8.76%,    │
│ the company is generating returns that barely match—or potentially sit      │
│ below—its weighted average cost of capital (WACC) once factoring in the     │
│ heavy debt servicing and equity dilution risks of a capital-intensive       │
│ conglomerate.                                                               │
│     * **The Conglomerate Discount Trap:** Investors cheering an 18x Forward │

│ systematically funneled into speculative, low-yielding greenfield ventures  │
│ (New Energy) with multi-decade payback horizons. Buying an asset because it │
│ looks "cheap" on forward earnings while its core capital efficiency is      │
